## Today

An order that goes wrong is handled entirely inside Shopify, and Grade10's
own order record never learns about it. The buyer's order history shows a
paid order forever; points earned on a refunded sale stay earned; the person
at the counter has no story to tell.

## The intended shape

A refund recorded where the money moves — Shopify — lands on Grade10's order
of record too: the order history says refunded, and the loyalty programme
claws back what the refunded spend earned under the same rules that granted
it. A return of a physical card is an operator move with a record, not an
email thread.

Nothing here is specified yet. This page marks the roadmap slot; the propose
action on it is where the requirements start.
