A collector who bids on six listings closing across one evening holds their
auction activity in browser tabs and memory. The only page carrying a
listing's standing is the listing's own; winning is learned from the operator
who makes contact. This capability gives the account one record of every lot
the collector bid on or watched, and where each stands.

The aftermath is the sharp end. Every post-sale fact a winner needs — outcome,
payment state, shipment state — already exists as an operator-side record the
person it is about cannot see; the record surfaces them to their owner. A
losing bidder's card authorization is released asynchronously and today
nothing says so, which reads on a bank statement as a charge for a lot they
did not win — the record says what is held, and when it was let go.
