title: Grade10
summary: One platform behind every storefront, read in one place.
---

Grade10 is one commerce platform wearing several faces. A storefront sells
goods, an auction house runs timed sales, a custody vault takes valuables in
and lends against them, a loyalty programme gives something back to the people
who keep coming, and a marketing site fronts all of it. Every brand assembles
from those same parts and one shared sign-in, so a person is the same person
wherever they turn up.

Read it product first. A product's landing page says what it is and who it
serves; under it sits a page per capability — one thing the product does, for
one audience. Conventions every product inherits rather than reaches for, like
how money is written and which zone a date is stated in, live under Platform.
How we work lives under Guides.

A page here is narrative and nothing else. The requirements are embedded
straight from the spec that governs them, the work in flight shows up on the
page it touches with its tasks done over total, and the visuals come from Figma
and the deployed Storybook. Nothing is copied, so a page cannot quietly drift
from what the platform actually does.

:::callout{kind="note"}
Every page is editable in the browser, and every edit lands in git — a commit
when you are running the manual locally, a pull request on your own branch when
you are on the hosted site. Git is the only state this app has.
:::
