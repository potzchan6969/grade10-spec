A collector keeps delivery addresses on their account and confirms one onto
each auction order they win. The book is account-wide and shared across
storefronts; [Winner Order](/p/grade10-site/auction/winner-order) holds the
form, the cap and the deadline that use it.

- **Saved addresses** — named addresses kept for future orders, capped at 5
- **One-time address** — an address confirmed onto one order without saving it
- **Fields** — the address form and which of its fields are required
- ❓ **Managing the book** — where a collector adds, edits and removes a saved address outside an order, so a full book can be cleared before an address deadline; Product and Design confirm
- ❓ **Store checkout** — whether the store's checkout address and this book are one set; Product confirms
