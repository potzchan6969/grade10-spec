`/about` is the company's page: who backs Grade10, what it does, where it is
going, and the parent company's record. It sits inside the same shell as
every surface — [Page Shell](/p/grade10-site/site/page-shell) — and answers
without a session, like any public surface —
[Crawlable Pages](/p/grade10-site/site/crawlable-pages).

## The Page

- 🚧 **Heading and introduction** — About Us as the page's heading beside
  a paragraph naming MemeStrategy and 9GAG
- 🚧 **What we do and where we're going** — two short statements, side by
  side on a wide viewport and stacked on a narrow one
- 🚧 **Market indexes** — the four index pages highlighted ahead of the
  parent-company panel, each opening in a new tab
- 🚧 **Parent company** — a quiet panel with the MemeStrategy mark, a
  paragraph on the company, and its milestones newest first, the upcoming
  one labelled Coming soon
- 🚧 **Links out** — MemeStrategy, 9GAG and Grade10 AI open in a new tab;
  Grade10 opens the front door; vaulting services and Grade10 Finance are
  plain text until they launch

- **Navigation entry** — About sits in the footer's Company column; the
  header lists no item for the page and marks nothing current
- ❓ Product - **Grade10 Finance destination** - the mention is plain text
  until the service launches; what it links to then is unconfirmed
- ❓ Copy - **Parent-company wording** - the paragraph was tightened after a
  copywriting review; whether it must reproduce issuer boilerplate verbatim
  is unconfirmed
