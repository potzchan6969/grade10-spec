The papers a vaulted card collects, from deposit to withdrawal. A paper is a
PDF kept outside the platform, for contracts and the accounts; a record is the
platform's own entry, read in the collector's dashboard.

## Papers

- 🚧 **Vault Terms** — signed by the collector at the first deposit: free storage, the incentive rules (the rate, eligibility, the daily drip, the day forfeited on withdrawal) and the withdrawal terms - [Vault Deposit](/p/grade10-site/vault/deposit#deposit-form)
- 🚧 **Custody Receipt** — each deposit: the cards, where they are held, their condition against the photos; confirmed by the collector and recorded by staff
- 🚧 **Incentive Ledger** — daily, per card: the price used, the liquidity factor, the rate, the amount earned and the amount cashed out - [Incentive](/p/grade10-site/vault/incentive)
- 🚧 **Consignment Agreement** — on consigning, signed by the collector and Grade10: the cards, the channel, the commission, the lowest acceptable price, the prices and the period - [Consigning](/p/grade10-site/consignment/consigning#terms)
- **Settlement Statement** — each period, per card: the sale price, the commission and the net, and the wallet's movement - [Earnings Wallet](/p/grade10-site/account/earnings-wallet#sale-proceeds)
  - **Commission Invoice** — with each statement: Grade10's details, its number and the commission total
  - **Credit Note** — for a refund after settlement: the commission reversed and the original invoice's number
- 🚧 **Withdrawal Note** — when a card is handed back: the cards, the ID checked, the condition and the day's incentive forfeited; confirmed by whoever collects and recorded by staff - [Vault Withdrawal](/p/grade10-site/vault/vault-withdrawal#collection)

## Rules

- **Never Edited** — a paper is made from stored data; a correction is a new version
- 🚧 **Records** — timestamped, never edited, and exported to PDF from the dashboard

:::detail{title="Product decisions" for="pm"}
For the collector and for the accounts, a card's history must read the same
whenever it is opened.

- **A correction is a new version** — an edited paper would leave two
  parties holding different terms under one name
- **Papers outside the platform** — contracts and the accounts are kept where
  finance and legal already keep them; the dashboard holds the platform's
  records
:::
