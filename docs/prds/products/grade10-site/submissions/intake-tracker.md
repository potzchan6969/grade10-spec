Where a collector follows the items they have handed in, from registration
at the counter until each one is in the vault or back from the grader.

- **Who** — a signed-in collector, reading only their own items
- **What starts it** — staff registering an item at the counter puts it on
  the tracker
- **Tabs** — In Progress, Completed and All; an item is complete once it
  has left intake, vaulted or handed to grading
- **Each item** — its name and grade, declared value, stage, the service it
  came in for (Vault, Grading, Auction Listing or Store Listing), its
  submission, and how, where and when it was handed in
- **From the portfolio** — the active intake alert on the portfolio opens
  the tracker
- ❓ **The design** — no frame is drawn yet; the workbench screen stands in
  until one is
