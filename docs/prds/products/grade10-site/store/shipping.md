## Today

The store sells physical cards and says nothing about how they travel.
Shopify takes the address at checkout; after that the buyer's order history
has no shipment state, no tracking, and no answer to the only question a
buyer has between paying and holding the card.

## Intended shape

The order of record carries a shipment state the order page and the order
history both render, a tracking reference lands on the order when the parcel
does, and pickup at the physical shop is a first-class choice rather than a
note in an email. The auction's post-sale queue is the operator half of the
same story; the store's version should not invent a second vocabulary for
it.

Nothing here is specified yet. This page marks the roadmap slot; the propose
action on it is where the requirements start.
