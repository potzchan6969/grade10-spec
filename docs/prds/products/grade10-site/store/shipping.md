## Today

Shopify takes the address at checkout. Your Orders and Order Details show an
order as Shipped once a parcel has left - [Order Status](/p/grade10-site/commerce/order-status) -
and offer a Track link when the Store holds a carrier address that is safe to
open - [Your Orders](/p/grade10-site/store/order-history). No badge says a
parcel arrived.

## Intended shape

Pickup at the physical shop is a first-class choice rather than a note in an
email. The auction's Orders workspace is the operator half of the same story;
the store's version should not invent a second vocabulary for it.

Nothing under Intended shape is specified yet. This page marks its roadmap slot; the propose
action on it is where the requirements start.
