A refund is money going back, and everything that rode on it going back with it.
Staff take it in Shopify; the collector's order, their points and their coupons
follow from there.

- **Where it starts** — Shopify, always. A collector has no action that starts
  one, and the store never refunds on its own
- **What the collector sees** — the order reads Refunded, in full or in part,
  and the amount sits beside what they paid
- **What comes back to them** — the money the shop sent, on the card or wallet
  that paid
- **What comes back to the programme** — points earned on the goods returned,
  and the points tender the return undoes
  1. **Earning** — clawed back for the goods that came back, never for the
     delivery, and never for goods that earned nothing
  2. **The tender** — all of it or none, because points were never allocated to
     a line anyone could return. A return of everything the points paid for
     gives them all back; a partial return leaves the member what they bought
     with
- **What it is priced on** — the goods the refund names, read against what the
  order earned on. A refund naming no goods gets its share of them instead, and
  the estimate is counted where an operator can find it
- **Money cannot come back that never went out** — each running total stops at
  what the order took, and the excess is said out loud

❓ **A mixed sale's return is priced by share, not by line** — an order that sold
a gift card beside graded cards prices a return as a fraction of both, so
returning the gift card alone claws back some earning and returning the cards
alone does not return the whole tender. The change that fixes it prices a refund
against the lines the order earned on.

❓ **A physical card coming back** — an operator's move with a record of its own.
