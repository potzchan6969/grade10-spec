---
title: Ending a Consignment
order: 2
---

The collector takes a card off sale before it sells. The card stays in the
vault and keeps earning.

## Request

- 🚧 **Who** — the collector from their dashboard, or staff for them at the counter
- 🚧 **In the Store** — while the card is on sale; not once it is sold or in an open order
- 🚧 **At Auction** — until the lot is published in the catalogue, then locked so bidders never see a lot disappear
- 🚧 **Early Fee** — where one is charged, it comes from the wallet; a short wallet is paid at the counter or becomes a debt

:::callout{kind="note"}
- ❓ **Fee for ending early** — none, a fixed fee a card, or a fee only once an auction lot is prepared. Product confirms
:::

## What Happens

- 🚧 **Off Sale at Once** — the store product is made unavailable without waiting for a stock sync; an auction lot is removed and staff are told
- 🚧 **Back to Vault-Only** — the card stays where it is, unless it was at an event, and keeps earning
- **Recorded** — the dashboard keeps the date and the reason

## Unsold at Auction

- 🚧 **No Sale** — the lot didn't sell, or the winner didn't pay and it isn't relisted: back to vault-only; the collector consigns it again or withdraws it

:::detail{title="Product decisions" for="pm"}
Ending a consignment should cost the collector as little as consigning did, so
it never needs a visit.

- **Locked once the catalogue is published** — bidders never see a lot disappear
- **A card off sale is a vault card again** — it keeps earning, so ending a consignment is never a loss of the incentive
:::
