The operator half of appointments. A shop is opened here, given its desks and
rooms, its hours and the services each can take; a day is read by resource;
a visit is booked, moved, cancelled and closed out on a collector's behalf.

The collector's half, booking and managing a visit, is
[Appointments](/p/grade10-site/appointment), and the rules both halves obey
are [`shared/appointment/scheduling`](/p/shared/appointment/scheduling).
