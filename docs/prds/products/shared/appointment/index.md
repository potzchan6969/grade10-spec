`shared-appointment` is the diary contract every brand's appointment service
is held to, and every product that books a visit. It is not a surface a
person opens: collectors reach it through [Book a Visit](/p/grade10-site/appointment/booking),
operators through the [Diary](/p/grade10-admin/appointment/diary), and a
product over its service binding.

One capability carries it, [scheduling](/p/shared/appointment/scheduling):
services, shops, resources, hours, how a slot is derived, how a booking is
held and moved, what a product may ask, and what a booking tells its owner.
