# Writing style

Read `docs/governance/writing.md` first. It is the standard, distilled from `docs/references/grade10-loyalty-program.md`, and short enough to hold whole.

Before handoff, hold the draft to it:

1. The opening paragraph says what the document is and what the reader leaves with.
2. Every decided fact reads flat and present-tense; every open item is marked ❓ or TBD on its own line.
3. Sections group one concern each; enumerable facts sit in tables, reasoning in prose.
4. Nothing recaps the change that produced the text — no today's gap, intended fix, or success metric outside a change proposal.
5. Nothing restates a testable statement from `openspec/specs/`; the prose distills and links.
6. Superseded content is replaced, never annotated.

A draft that fails one of these gets rewritten, not footnoted.
