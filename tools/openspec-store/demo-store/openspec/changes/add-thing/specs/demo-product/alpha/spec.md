## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The thing is watched

The system SHALL let a reader watch the thing.

#### Scenario: alpha-SC-03 - A reader watches the thing

- **WHEN** a reader watches the thing
- **THEN** the watch is recorded

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: The thing happens once

The system SHALL do the thing exactly once, and SHALL say so.

#### Scenario: alpha-SC-01 - The thing happens

- **WHEN** a reader asks for the thing
- **THEN** the thing happens and the system says so
