## User journeys

### grade10-site-ticketing-buying-US-01: Guest buys passes without an account

**As a** visitor who has never made an account,
**I want** to buy show passes with just my name and email,
**so that** nothing stands between deciding to go and having tickets.

### grade10-site-ticketing-buying-US-02: Staff rehearse the sale before the public

**As an** operator,
**I want** the show on sale on the real site to staff and our own domains
only,
**so that** we walk the whole purchase, mail and door before the public
can buy.

### grade10-site-ticketing-buying-US-03: Buyer skips the entrance queue

**As a** buyer,
**I want** to upgrade my passes with Fast Pass for the days I want it,
**so that** I skip the queue on those days.
