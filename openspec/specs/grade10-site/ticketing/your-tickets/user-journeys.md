## User journeys

### grade10-site-ticketing-your-tickets-US-01: Buyer finds the tickets in the mail

**As a** buyer,
**I want** my tickets in my mailbox, one per page,
**so that** I can show mine at the door and send each friend theirs without
signing in anywhere.

### grade10-site-ticketing-your-tickets-US-02: Guest sees their orders after signing in

**As a** guest buyer,
**I want** my orders and points waiting when I first sign in with my email,
**so that** buying as a guest loses me nothing.
