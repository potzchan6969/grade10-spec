## User journeys

### grade10-site-loyalty-membership-page-US-01: Member reads standing and acts from one screen

**As a** member,
**I want** my tier, balance, rewards, coupons and points history on one screen,
with the member pass and the top-up entrance beside the title,
**so that** I can read my standing and act on it without leaving the page.

## Context user journeys

### grade10-site-loyalty-programme-US-04: Member reads two counts and redeems from one surface

**As a** member,
**I want** my tier, balance, and activity on one surface, in the programme's dates,
**so that** I can join, redeem, and never see the operator's reasons behind an entry.

This page is that surface: its panes compose the programme's exports.

### grade10-site-loyalty-programme-US-14: Member buys a points pack

**As a** member,
**I want** to pick a points pack from my membership surface and pay for it,
**so that** I can top up a balance that will not cover what I want.

The journey lands in `add-top-up-pack-card`; this page is where it plays
out — the picker and confirming wait over the page, the outcome as a
toast, the held pack's link into Points History.
