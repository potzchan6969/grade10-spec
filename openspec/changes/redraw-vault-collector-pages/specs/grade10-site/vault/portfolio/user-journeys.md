## ADDED User journeys

### grade10-site-vault-portfolio-US-01: Collector reviews their vaulted holdings

**As a** collector,
**I want** to see every item I hold in the vault with its estimate,
**so that** I know what is stored and what it is worth.

### grade10-site-vault-portfolio-US-02: Collector reads one vaulted item

**As a** collector,
**I want** to open one vaulted item and read its status, estimate, grade and certification, storage location, condition, custody history and documents,
**so that** I can verify what the vault holds for me.

### grade10-site-vault-portfolio-US-03: Collector verifies a graded item's certification

**As a** collector,
**I want** to open the grading institute's own record for my item's certificate,
**so that** I can confirm the grade against the source.

### grade10-site-vault-portfolio-US-04: Collector lists a vaulted item

**As a** collector,
**I want** to list a vaulted item for auction or for sale without taking it home first,
**so that** I can sell it while it stays in custody.

### grade10-site-vault-portfolio-US-05: Collector submits a raw vaulted item for grading

**As a** collector,
**I want** to send a raw vaulted item to grading from its page,
**so that** its graded value can be realized without a return trip.

### grade10-site-vault-portfolio-US-06: Collector requests a vaulted item back

**As a** collector,
**I want** to ask for a vaulted item to be returned to me,
**so that** I can take it out of custody when I want it back.
