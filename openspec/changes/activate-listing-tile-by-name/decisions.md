## Goals

- A collector who taps a product's name on a listing tile opens the product,
  as the photo already does.
- A tile that sells keeps a sold-out product's name and photo inert.
- A Tab user meets one stop per tile to open its product.

## Non-Goals

- Navigation inside the package - the consumer decides route or modal.
- Always-visible link chrome - the underline on hover and on keyboard focus is the cue.
- Changing cart or price behaviour.
- Adaptive Filter chrome - `adapt-listing-filter-drawer`.

## Decisions

| Q | Asked | Decided | Instead of |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | What does the name do when pressed? | Reports the same tile activation as the photo, through the one callback the tile already takes. Carried by the tile requirement **The product list displays product tiles and delegates every product action** and the page line **Name opens the product** - the proposal, @tangconst, 2026-09-11 | A second callback for the name - two ways to say one thing |
| Q2 | What shows that the name opens? | An underline on hover and on keyboard focus; the Figma Product Card frame stays photo, plain name and prices. Carried by the tile requirement's underline rule and the page's Product decisions row **The underline means it opens** - the proposal, @tangconst, 2026-09-11 | A name drawn as a link at rest - chrome the design does not draw |
| Q3 | Does a sold-out product's name open? | Not where the tile sells - a tile given a cart handler keeps a sold-out name and photo inert; where the tile does not sell, a sold-out tile opens, since a surface that carries the collector on rather than selling has no reason to stop at a card nobody can buy. Carried by the tile requirement's rule for when a tile opens, and the page lines **Opens where it does not sell** and **Name opens the product** - decided by @ecchochan, 2026-10-05 | Always inert - a dead end on a surface that exists to carry the collector on |
| Q4 | What does a tile with no activation callback and no address do? | Its name and photo stay inert. Carried by the tile requirement **The product list displays product tiles and delegates every product action** and the page line **Name opens the product** - the proposal, @tangconst, 2026-09-11 | A default navigation inside the package - the consumer owns where a product opens |
| Q5 | Which keys open the name? | The keys its native control takes: Enter and Space on a button, Enter on a link. Carried by the tile requirement's keyboard rule and the page line **One stop to open** - decided by @ecchochan, 2026-10-05 | Enter alone on both - a button that ignores Space is not a button |
| Q6 | Does the keyboard stop on both the photo and the name? | On the name alone: the name is the tile's one keyboard stop that opens the product and the one control a screen reader announces for it; the photo opens on a pointer press and is no stop, so a Tab user passes one stop fewer on every tile and each product is announced once. The cart control keeps its own stop. Carried by the tile requirement's keyboard rule, the page line **One stop to open** and its Product decisions row - decided by @ecchochan, 2026-10-05 | Two stops for one destination, the product announced twice |
| Q7 | Does a name that does not open take the underline? | No: it is plain text, with no underline and no focus, so the underline only ever means the name opens. Carried by the tile requirement's underline rule and the page's Product decisions row **The underline means it opens** - decided by @ecchochan, 2026-10-05 | The underline on every name - a cue that promises a press that does nothing |
| Q8 | Does a tile given its product's address and no activation callback open? | Yes, as a link: a tile that has an address is a link to it, and a plain press follows it with nothing reported. This change carries the rule: its tile requirement opens a tile given a callback or an address, and keeps it inert where it has neither; `add-store-cross-sell`'s requirement that a tile given an address is a link to it says how the link behaves. Carried by the page line **Name opens the product** - decided by @ecchochan, 2026-10-05 | Inert without a callback, address or not - a link that does not open |
| Q9 | Where does the rule that a sold-out tile opens where it does not sell live? | In the tile requirement this change modifies, with its scenario, since it is part of when any tile opens; `add-store-cross-sell`'s requirement "A sold-out tile still opens where its activation is handled" and its scenario leave that change's delta, so the durable spec states the rule once - decided by the round | The rule in two requirements, one from each change |
| Q10 | Is a tile always given its product's name? | Yes: the name is a supplied fact the tile requires as text, the name a screen reader reads the tile as (`ProductCardProps.name`, `packages/ui/src/blocks/store-product-listing/product-card.tsx:30`), and the catalogue authors a title for every product. A tile with no name is outside the contract, as one with no price is. Carried by the tile requirement **The product list displays product tiles and delegates every product action**, which lists the name among each product's supplied facts; the shared suite's Settled records it - decided by the round | The photo as the stop where the name is empty - a second rule for a tile no surface draws |
| Q11 | Do **Responsive layout** and **Load more** stay their own parts of the capability's map? | Yes, both, as the durable spec places them: no other part means either, and folding them changes nothing a shopper meets while it moves anchors `adapt-listing-filter-drawer` serves. The delta restates both groups as they stand. Carried by the page's Product decisions rows **Responsive layout** and **Load more**; the same answer closes `add-store-cross-sell`'s Q57 - the product owner's word, taking the recommendation, 2026-10-07 | Each folded into a part that already exists - Tile contract, Filters and sort |
| Q12 | Does the product page's rule that the storefront opens a card from the grid hold for a sold-out card? | Only where the card opens. The product page keeps the card's own address and the no-page-load rule; which cards open, and from which control, is the surface's rule - the listing's for the listing, where a sold-out card opens neither (Q3), the front door's for its row, and the cross-sell rail's for the rail under a card. The listing page's **Every product** line says the same - decided by the round | The product page's rule unconditional beside the listing's, the same fact stated two ways |
| Q13 | Which of the page's outcomes does this change build? | One stop to open, and the photo on its own opening by the tile's rule (Q14). The name that opens and stays plain where it does not, and a sold-out tile that opens where it does not sell, already run: `ProductCard` computes when the tile opens and draws the name as its control (`packages/ui/src/blocks/store-product-listing/product-card.tsx:122-165`, grade10's pinned `external/grade10-spec` c1a6d0286); the listing passes a cart handler and `onProductClick` (`apps/frontend/grade10/src/pages/store/ProductListingPage.tsx:571-581`), and the front door's row passes `onClick` and no cart handler (`apps/frontend/grade10/src/pages/store/StoreHomePage.tsx:213-233`). The pages show those lines unmarked; this change states them as requirements and its walk proves them - decided by the round, 2026-10-06 | 🚧 on outcomes that run, the page reading them as not yet built |
| Q14 | When does a `ProductCardImage` used without a card open? | Under the tile's rule: given an activation callback or the product's address, unless the product is sold out and a cart handler is supplied. `ProductCard` and the image compute it in one place, and the image's one control is named by the product name it is given. Carried by the image requirement **The product card image displays photo, sale, sold-out, and cart overlay** and the page line **Photo on its own** - decided by the round, 2026-10-06 | The image opening wherever it is given a callback or an address (`product-card-image.tsx:92`), a second rule for one fact |
| Q15 | Is a listing card's name a link to its product's address, or a button that opens it in place? | A button: the listing gives its cards a callback and no address (`apps/frontend/grade10/src/pages/store/ProductListingPage.tsx:575`), so a card's name opens on Enter and Space (Q5) and is not a link (Q8). The listing gives its tiles their addresses in its own round, as Product Listing Blocks' Product decisions row **Tile as a link** records - decided by the round, 2026-10-06 | Addresses on the listing in this change - a second outcome beside one stop to open |
| Q16 | Where does a card's page start when it opens without a page load? | At its top: a navigation to a new entry starts at the top, by `grade10-site/site/navigation`'s requirement **Navigation keeps the collector's place**, which runs on every surface (`apps/frontend/grade10/src/root.tsx:177`). The product page states no rule of its own - decided by the round, 2026-10-06 | A product-page rule for it - the site's rule stated twice |
| Q17 | What does this change measure, given a card reports one open and names no control? | Product opens from the listing: Product Viewed with Source `Listing` or `Search`, which the site already sends for every card the listing opens (`apps/frontend/grade10/src/pages/store/ProductPage.tsx:84-87`, `apps/frontend/grade10/src/pages/store/ProductListingPage.tsx:575-580`, `packages/grade10-store/backend/src/services/analytics/events.ts:43-47`). The share before and after one stop to open shows whether it lifts listing opens, and nothing new is built. Carried by Product Listing's Measurement signal **Product opens from the listing** - the product owner's word, taking the recommendation, 2026-10-07 | The share of opens that start from the name, which nothing can read; or a report of which control was pressed, which widens the export contract Q1 keeps narrow |
