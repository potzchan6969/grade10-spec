### Questions

1. **Lowering a Not placed internal bid once the lot is open** - After its time, while the lot takes bids, may the admin lower a Not placed internal bid (it stays Not placed), and is a raise still short of the minimum next bid refused?
   - **A - Lower accepted, short raise refused - Recommended** - Matches Q11, SC-11, SC-30 and US5-TC4-1. The requirement's item 1 and tech-design Decision 6 and its routing table change; Q28 is reworded to "any amount the Save rules allow while created; once open, a save at or above the minimum next bid places it, a lower stays Not placed, and a short raise is refused".
   - **B - Every save below the minimum next bid refused once open** - Matches the requirement and tech design as written. Q11's "can be lowered" narrows to before publishing, and SC-30 and US5-TC4-1 are rewritten as refusals.
   - **Why it matters** - The admin either can or cannot record a lower floor on a lot that ran past it; the case and the build currently disagree.
   - **Blocks** - Acceptance of `add-auction-internal-bid`; task 5.1/5.2 tests for SC-30 and SC-11.
   - **Reasons** - Q11: "A raise still short of the minimum next bid is refused. A Not placed internal bid can be lowered or removed" (`decisions.md:41`); requirement item 1: "a save below it is refused, naming the minimum next bid" (`specs/grade10-admin/auction/internal-bid/spec.md:371-374`).
