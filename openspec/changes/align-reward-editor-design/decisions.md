## Goals

- An operator creates a free item in two choices: Free item, then one variant.
- The reward editor, the rewards list and every Grade10 admin page take the
  approved mock's controls, type and words.
- Nothing staging's editor can do is lost.

## Non-Goals

- What a coupon takes off, or what saving sends to the backend.
- A new theme for the ZZZ admin: it keeps Stone, and the shared controls take
  their new shape wherever it renders them.
- How a table cell writes an amount.
- A dark admin: the brand states light only.
- Thumbnails in the picked list: the catalogue search carries no image.
- Whether a free item spent online ships free or is collection only:
  Product's open question on Rewards · Using a Reward.

## Decisions

| Q | Asked | Decided | Instead of |

| Q1 | How does an operator author a free item? | Free item is its own choice: one variant at 100% off with no maximum discount, saved as the product coupon Money off saves - the proposal, @ecchochan, 2026-09-14. Stated on Operator Console · Authoring a Reward, the Free item line, and in "The console's reward form authors a reward's full definition" | `Everything (free)` under Money off, then Named variants, then a variant - four choices for the most common physical reward |
| Q2 | Which choice does a stored reward open on? | A product coupon at 100%, with no maximum discount, scoped to exactly one variant opens as Free item, when opened and when duplicated; any other product coupon opens as Money off - the proposal, @ecchochan, 2026-09-14. Stated on Operator Console · Authoring a Reward, the Free item line, and in "The console's reward form authors a reward's full definition" | Opening on the choice that authored it - the coupon does not record it, and both choices save the same coupon |
| Q3 | Must a percentage carry a maximum discount? | No: the maximum discount is optional, as the console already saves it - the proposal, @ecchochan, 2026-09-14. Stated on Rewards · Reward Types and in "A reward names a kind, a discount and a scope" | A percentage capped at a maximum discount, as the durable requirement read |
| Q4 | Where is a reward scoped to named products or a catalog filter good? | Online only, whatever channels the coupon names: a sale at the till names its goods by variant alone. The console saves such a reward for online alone (grade10 `packages/loyalty/admin-frontend/src/features/programme/rewards/presentation/rewardCouponDraft.ts:153-166`), and the till's panel shows it as online only (`packages/grade10-store/backend/src/services/pos/sale/sale.ts:606-609`), where staff cannot apply it (`integrations/shopify-pos/grade10/src/acts/view.ts:221`). The till also refuses it from the member's own phone, and the member's reward menu states it as online only before the points go; these two are still to build, since the programme's guard and the menu read the stored channels alone (`packages/loyalty/contracts/src/couponGuard.ts:14`) - the acceptance review, 2026-10-06, from what runs and from the page. Stated on Rewards · Reward Types and Reward Shop, Coupons · Validity and Refusals, Operator Console · Authoring a Reward, and in "A reward names a kind, a discount and a scope" | Those scopes at the till as well - the till cannot tell which lines they match |
| Q5 | What mark sits in front of a money field? | `HKD`, as built (grade10 `packages/frontend-console/src/MoneyField.tsx:103-104`): fields and console tables name the currency one way, the accessible name stays `Amount HKD`, and no code changes - @ecchochan, the product owner, 2026-10-07. Stated on Operator Console · Authoring a Reward, the Currency mark line and row | `HK$`, as the mock draws it, taken from `Intl.NumberFormat` `formatToParts`; the accessible name follows the mark, and `MoneyField` changes in grade10 |
| Q6 | Does the designer accept each departure from the mock in `ui-design.md`'s Differences From the Mock, the retired handover's note among them? | Each departure stands as written, as this change's interim; the dark badge text stands either way, since the mock's status text on its tint reads 3.2:1 and 2.4:1 against the 4.5:1 small text needs - the acceptance review, 2026-10-07. The designer confirms or redraws each in confirm-loyalty-surface-looks. Stated on Operator Console · Authoring a Reward, the Departures from the mock row | Overriding named items - each override is a new grade10 task under group 4, and US9-TC11 runs again |
| Q7 | Which channels does the form show for a reward scoped to named products or a catalog filter that is stored for the till as well, and what does saving it unchanged save? | Online chosen, with In store and Both unavailable; saving it unchanged saves it for online alone, and moving its scope to named variants or the whole order shows the channels it was stored with again. The form keeps the stored channels and shows the effective ones (grade10 `packages/loyalty/admin-frontend/src/features/programme/rewards/presentation/views/CouponTermsFields.tsx:62-63`, `rewardCouponDraft.ts:162-166`) - decided by the round at QA2, 2026-10-06, from what runs. Stated on Operator Console · Authoring a Reward, the Online only by product or filter line, and in "A reward names a kind, a discount and a scope" | Rewriting the channels to online when the reward opens, which loses the stored channels if the operator moves the scope back |
| Q8 | What does the form do with a reward stored with a handover the programme has retired - a manual handover or a counter collection? | It opens with no choice made and keeps that handover, unchanged, while the fields beside it are edited, and a duplicate of it opens as Money off (grade10 `packages/loyalty/admin-frontend/src/features/programme/rewards/presentation/rewardCouponDraft.ts:46-53`, `:193-201`) - decided by the round at the acceptance review of the rebased change, 2026-10-06, from what runs. Once the operator makes one of the three choices, it saves as that choice (`views/RewardEditor.tsx:275`, `rewardCouponDraft.ts:500-507`) - decided by the round at QA2 of the rebased change, 2026-10-06, from what runs. A kind is the coupon's, a product coupon or a gift; a handover is how the reward reaches the member (grade10 `packages/loyalty/contracts/src/schemas.ts`, `fulfillmentConfigSchema`). Stated on Operator Console · Authoring a Reward, the Retired handovers line, and in "The console's reward form authors a reward's full definition" | Opening it as Money off, which rewrites how the reward is handed over on its next save |
| Q9 | What does a choice open on when the operator moves to it from another? | Nothing picked or entered: Money off opens on an amount, none entered, and named variants, none picked; Free item and Gift with a purchase open with no variant picked. Nothing the choice before held carries over (grade10 `packages/loyalty/admin-frontend/src/features/programme/rewards/presentation/rewardCouponDraft.ts:89-102`, `views/RewardEditor.tsx:274-276`) - decided by the round at QA2, 2026-10-07, from what runs. Stated on Operator Console · Authoring a Reward, the A new choice starts empty line, and in "The console's reward form authors a reward's full definition" | Money off opening on Everything (free), Named variants and the free item's variant, which keeps the operator's pick and reopens as a Free item once saved |
| Q10 | What else does a Free item show, and does a stored free item keep its combine setting? | Every choice shows the coupon's terms - validity, channels and combine setting - and the menu entry with its stock and window; a stored free item reopens with its combine setting, and saving it unchanged keeps it (`views/RewardEditor.tsx:318-329`, `rewardCouponDraft.ts:213`, `:452-458`) - decided by the round at QA2, 2026-10-07, from what runs. Stated in "The console's reward form authors a reward's full definition", which sets the combine setting for every reward | A Free item with no combine setting of its own, which would drop one stored on the reward |
| Q11 | Is a window that starts and ends on the same day saved? | Yes: Live from and Live until are days, from the start of the first to the end of the last, so a one-day window is in order (`rewardGaps.ts:155-166`) - decided by the round at QA2, 2026-10-07, from what runs, and the mock's own `Inclusive.` hint on Live until. Stated on Operator Console · Authoring a Reward, the Missing parts line, and in "The console's reward form authors a reward's full definition"; worded on Live until in `ui-design.md`'s Copy, the Window row | Refusing it as a window that does not end after it starts |
| Q12 | What does a second pick do in the one-variant picker of a Free item or a gift? | It replaces the first, so the picker never holds two (grade10 `packages/frontend-console/src/SearchPicker.tsx:38`, `:137`; `views/HandoverFields.tsx:82-97`) - decided by the round at QA2, 2026-10-07, from what runs. Stated in `ui-design.md`'s States | Refusing the second pick until the first is removed |
| Q13 | What does the rail show for a reward stored with a retired handover, until a choice is made? | The menu card shows the reward as the member's menu does: its name, description, cost and stock, a counter collection's days to collect, and nothing for a manual handover. The sentence reads the stored handover, and the basket check asks for the coupon to be finished (`views/ReadBackRail.tsx:49-58`, `basketVerdict.ts:89`; the member's menu `packages/loyalty/frontend/src/features/programme/rewards/presentation/projections.ts:86-98`) - decided by the round at QA2, 2026-10-07, from what runs, bar the card's limits, which read the form's 30-day validity (`rewardCouponDraft.ts:179-201`, `rewardCopy.ts:207-226`) and are fixed in tasks 6.10. Stated in `ui-design.md`'s States | A card reading the form's defaults, which tells the operator the member sees a 30-day coupon the reward does not issue |
