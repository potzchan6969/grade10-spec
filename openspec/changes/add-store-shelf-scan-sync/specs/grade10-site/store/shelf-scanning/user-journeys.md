## ADDED User journeys

### g10.store-shelf-scanning.US1: Shop staff scan a shelf product and see what the store sells it as

**As a** shopkeeper on the shop floor,
**I want** to point the scanner at a product on the shelf and see the store's
title, price and stock for it,
**so that** I can answer a customer without looking the product up.

### g10.store-shelf-scanning.US2: Shop staff open a scanned product's store page

**As a** shopkeeper on the shop floor,
**I want** to open the store page of the product the scanner names,
**so that** I can show the customer its photos and details or help them buy
it online.

### g10.store-shelf-scanning.US3: Shop staff scan a slab and see its own listing

**As a** shopkeeper on the shop floor,
**I want** the scanner, reading a slab's label, to name the one store product
that sells that slab, with its grade and cert,
**so that** I quote the price of the copy in my hand, not another copy of the
same card.

### g10.store-shelf-scanning.US4: Shop staff scan a sold-out product and are told it is sold out

**As a** shopkeeper on the shop floor,
**I want** a product the store has sold out of to still be named, and to read
"Sold out",
**so that** I can tell a customer it is gone rather than that the scanner does
not know it.

### g10.store-shelf-scanning.US5: Stock keeper sees a catalogue change on the scanner

**As a** stock keeper,
**I want** a product I publish, reprice, sell out or take down on the shop to
reach the scanner within minutes, and its new photos within the hour,
**so that** the shop floor never quotes a price or a product the store no
longer has.
