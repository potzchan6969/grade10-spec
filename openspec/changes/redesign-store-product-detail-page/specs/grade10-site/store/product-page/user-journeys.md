## User journeys

### grade10-site-store-product-page-US-06: Collector reviews a product's catalogue context

**As a** collector,
**I want** a product page to show the product's supplied images, price context,
and item facts,
**so that** I can understand what is available before I decide to buy.

**Accepted by:**

- `grade10-site-store-product-page-SC-13` — A product page shows its media and price context
- `grade10-site-store-product-page-SC-14` — A product without media has an honest placeholder
- `grade10-site-store-product-page-SC-15` — A product page shows supplied item facts

### grade10-site-store-product-page-US-07: Collector expands the product description in place

**As a** collector,
**I want** to expand and collapse a long product description where it is shown,
**so that** I can read the full description without leaving the product page.

**Accepted by:**

- `grade10-site-store-product-page-SC-16` — A collector expands and collapses the description

### grade10-site-store-product-page-US-08: Collector adds a chosen quantity from the product page

**As a** collector,
**I want** to choose a quantity and add it from the product page,
**so that** I can buy the quantity I chose while staying on the product.

**Accepted by:**

- `grade10-site-store-product-page-SC-17` — A collector adds a chosen quantity in place

### grade10-site-store-product-page-US-09: Collector meets a product with no variant for sale

**As a** collector,
**I want** a product with no available variant to keep its prices and say that it is sold out,
**so that** I can tell an unavailable product from a broken purchase page.

**Accepted by:**

- `grade10-site-store-product-page-SC-18` — A sold-out product offers no add action
