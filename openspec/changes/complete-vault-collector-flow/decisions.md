## Goals

- A collector answers an offer, cancels a request and asks for the item back from their own case page, each act behind a confirmation naming what it does
- A collector reads the fact their case meets — a lapsed, declined or superseded offer, a missed visit, an ask for the item back, and the four endings — in their own words, derived at the read, with a stepper and a chip saying where the case stands and whose the item is
- A case carries a six-character reference a person can say at the counter, type as the bank-transfer reference and search by in the console
- A borrower with a live loan reads where to pay, each repayment and what it did, the reminder dates, and the final notice with its date to pay by
- A collector reviews the request and acknowledges the collection statement before sending it, and lands on a booked-visit screen they can put in their calendar
- A collector reads what the vault keeps about them, their identity standing, downloads every signed document at once, and files the ask to be forgotten from one page, cancelling it inside the window if they change their mind
- An operator reads the day's counts, visits, arrears and held items at a glance, sees the rule before the refusal on the three dialogs, ticks the agreement's terms, walks the visit in order and reads why Forfeit is not offered yet
- Every collector email names what its board names, as a table and blocks with the lender's footer, from letters the worker renders in step with the preview in this store
- The identity panel shows the record's six states, and the notifications requirement counts the twenty-four messages the worker sends

## Non-Goals

- A new status for any of the facts the page derives
- SMS or WhatsApp automation; click-to-chat stays staff-pressed
- A second custodian
- Online payment; the borrower pays at their own bank and a treasurer records it
- A signing-room resource in the diary; the vault books one visit and the signing happens inside it
- The operative legal wording counsel owes — the notice's clause, the licence line, the collection statement; it stays bracketed outside production
- Anything the grading product needs from the vault, which `add-card-grading` carries
- A locker registry, capacity, transfers or a stock-take against the shelf
- A forfeiture stepper; Forfeit is withheld with its reason in words

## Decisions

| Q | Asked | Decided | Instead of |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | What does the collector read when the offer lapsed, was declined or replaced, when a visit was missed, or when they asked for the item back? | The fact, derived at every read from the case, its offer and its visit, in the collector's words with the one thing to do next; the lapsed offer stops reading as a live one | A fifteenth status and its siblings — a cached answer that can be wrong, the reason `overdue` is no status either |
| Q2 | Accept, Decline, Cancel and Ask for it back are on the worker and in the catalogue and not on the page. Wire them, or leave them to the counter? | Wire them, each behind a confirmation naming the total, what a late day costs and what will be signed | The counter alone, which the record already rejects: the collector answers for themselves |
| Q3 | How does a case get a handle a person can use? | A six-character reference from an alphabet that cannot be misread, issued at intake beside the id, unique per brand across every case ever issued and never reused; intake reissues on a clash, the mechanism the tech design's; the id stays the key and the address | Shortening the id, which is the key and every email's address; a reference derived from the id, which cannot be reissued on a clash; uniqueness among live cases only, which hands a settled case's reference to a new borrower |
| Q4 | Which alphabet, and does the reference go in the address? | Digits and capitals without 0, O, 1, I and L; the address keeps the id - decided by the round | All 36 characters, which read 0 as O at a counter; the reference in the address, which would rewrite every email link |
| Q5 | How is a borrower told where to pay? | A structured block, one set everywhere: the lender's FPS id, its bank account under the lender's registered name already on file, the case reference as the transfer reference, or card or cash at the counter; on the live loan and in every money message, Finance owning the id and the account | One free-text instructions field, the standing decision, which nothing renders and no borrower can copy into a bank form; a fourth account-name field, which would duplicate the lender's legal name |
| Q6 | Where do the repayments and the final notice reach the borrower? | On the case page, with the allocation sentence, beside the email | Email only, which leaves the borrower re-reading a month of mail to know the balance |
| Q7 | What is the wizard's third step? | A review that reads the request back, says what happens next, and takes the tick that the collection statement was read | Sending from the photo step with the statement linked in a footer, which evidences nothing |
| Q8 | Does the tick wait for Legal's statement text? | No; it ships linking the statement page, which reads "Being prepared" until Legal writes it - decided by the round | Holding the step until the wording lands |
| Q9 | What happens after a visit is booked? | A booked screen naming the shop, the slot and what to bring, with add to calendar, move and cancel; add to calendar is a calendar file the phone opens, naming the visit, so a moved visit's file replaces the one before it and a cancellation withdraws it | Returning to the case; a calendar-provider link, which picks a provider for the collector; a fresh file per booking, which leaves the stale one on the phone |
| Q10 | Where does the collector read what is kept and ask to be forgotten? | One Your data page under the account: the retention table per class, the identity standing, every signed document in one download, the ask with its in-flight refusal in words | A retention card on the released case alone, with no download and no ask |
| Q11 | Does the arrears view need tiles when it already counts? | The view's count, plus the two figures the list cannot answer without summing it — outstanding in arrears and how many carry no notice; the arrears delta lands on `money-book`, whose list it sums | Four tiles, two of which restate the list — the count and the oldest row; the tiles on `operator-queue`, which does not own the arrears |
| Q12 | The canvas also draws a stock-take sheet and Send notice from an arrears row. In? | ❓ pm - recommended: neither; the sheet belongs to the deferred stock-take, and the notice stays an act on the case | Both as drawn on A02 and A08 |
| Q13 | Does an operator learn a rule from the refusal or before it? | Before it: the make-offer dialog shows the cap, the presets, the derived interest, total, late-day figure and annualised rate and the five gates; the vault dialog its three preconditions; the payout dialog the two people, the due date and the reminder days | The worker's refusal as the only teacher, which it stays behind the display |
| Q14 | How are the key terms recorded as explained? | The dialog prints the loan agreement's own terms from the `documents-and-signing` spec, each ticked before the loan packet opens, and records the one fact the spec already holds: explained, with the optional recording reference | A second list of five items kept in step with the agreement by hand; the lone reference field, which records that a call happened and not what it covered |
| Q15 | How does a counter of three run the visit? | The Case tab opens on the visit's steps in order, ticked as each act lands, saying why an act is not offered yet; on the Custody tab, Forfeit is withheld with its reason in words — not before the cure date, the notice sent on which day | A four-state forfeiture stepper, which retells what the withheld button and the cure date already say; flat action lists per tab, learned from a manual |
| Q16 | What do the emails carry, and where are they built? | Every message names what its board names — the terms table, the total, the late-day cost, the expiry, how to pay, the reminder schedule, the notice's clause, lapse condition and "a person decides" line, the licence footer and the complaints contact — as React Email letters the worker renders itself in `packages/vault/backend/src/email`, the auction's letter shape, aligned with the preview source in this store's `apps/emails`; the words stay one typed `LetterCopy` per kind in `email/messages.ts`, widened to the blocks its letter carries, and the letter composes the shell and blocks around them | Templates in `apps/emails` alone, which is a preview site and renders to nobody; copy moved to `@grade10/i18n` inside the four-field shell that cannot hold a table; retiring `messages.ts` for words written inside the letters, which no catalogue and no test could hold |
| Q17 | Do the emails wait for the licence wording? | No; production refuses the act that would print a value Legal owes — the forfeiture notice's send, a money email's licence footer, a minted document's licence line — while it is unset, and the act renders its letter before it commits, so nothing is written that cannot be posted; brackets print outside production only. The collection statement's tick is not one of those acts: it records the version it showed, as Q8 settled | Holding the templates until item 1 closes; bracketed values in production mail, where the notice is evidence; refusing the tick too, which would close production intake until Legal answers |
| Q18 | What does the identity panel show? | The record's six states: Verified, Out, Stalled, Refused, Lapsed, None | The provider's eight, which the console shows today and the PRD never defined |
| Q19 | The spec tables twenty-three messages and the worker sends twenty-four. Which is right? | Twenty-four; the identity-check invitation joins the table under `collector-notifications` | Tabling the invitation under `identity-verification`, which would split one closed set in two |
| Q20 | The canvas draws a signing-room resource in the diary. Does the vault book it? | No; the vault books one visit and the signing happens inside it | A second booking per case, which the visit-booking capability refuses |
| Q21 | One change, or three? | One change: the whole canvas is delivered by one team in one effort, so archive independence buys nothing; reviewability comes from the pull request and the task groups | Three changes off one frontier — collector flow, console, messages — which triples the specs, suites and task plans for one delivery and adds one ordering edge, the reference issued before the console searches on it |
| Q22 | The canvas draws an eight-stage stepper and an ownership chip on nearly every collector screen. In? | In: both derived from the status at the read, one 🚧 line on the case page | Deferring them, which ships every screen without the line that says where the case is |
| Q23 | Who files the ask to be forgotten? | The collector, from Your data, cancellable inside the seven days; a self-filed request bans nothing, so the person can still sign in to cancel; the vault answers for its own classes and refusal, and `shared/auth/users`, which owns the request write and the ban a console filing applies, joins the change | The admin-only path, where the collector reads the refusal in words and still asks an operator to file the request |
| Q24 | A CSV, or a finance report screen — and is a bulk download bounded? | A CSV of the ledger as filtered; every bulk download — the CSV and the collector's document set — bounded to what its list pages and written on the audit chain like a search: who, when, the filter, how many | A finance report screen apart from the ledger, a second reading of the same rows; an unbounded export that leaves no trail |
| Q25 | What is the net out of the business? | One fold: payouts less repayments, per currency, a correction netting the row it took back once, positive when money is out | A sum over every kind with a correction subtracted twice; one figure across currencies, which the position already refuses |
| Q26 | A kind filter on the ledger, or the method filter alone? | A kind filter — payout, repayment, correction — beside the method filter, so a controller reads one kind against the statement; the takes-back column and the per-kind totals are already required, so the console renders them and the delta names the filter | The method filter alone, which cannot separate an advance from a repayment by transfer |
| Q27 | Search by the reference, or the id prefix alone? | Prefix on the six-character reference, writing the same search row on the audit chain | Prefix on the uuid alone, which finds nothing a person can read out |
| Q28 | The Today block: a second surface over the Today cut? | The landing view opens on the Today cut's own query, in slot order with its count — one query, one surface | A Today block with a query of its own, which can disagree with the Today view across a midnight |
| Q29 | How long does the download offered at the seal live, and on which surface, when the case page already keeps a copy? | 15 minutes from the seal, on the ceremony's own screen; the email and the case page are the durable paths. The requirement *Every signer keeps a copy, three ways* already states it, and the blind pass was denied the requirements | A fourth copy path; a second duration written beside the one the requirement holds |
| Q30 | A packet lives a day, or until a day past the visit it belongs to. Which bound applies, and can both bear on one packet? | Both bear on it and the later one wins: 24 hours from preparation, or a day past the visit the case holds where that is later. The requirement *A packet is prepared as one set, for a case that is ready for it* already states it | One fixed day, which closes a packet prepared on Monday for Friday's visit |
| Q31 | Must the asker be signed in to verify a document's digest? | No. The vault answers at a public address taking the digest, and the answer carries nothing about the person, so the outside-the-business actor the blind cases use is the right one. The requirement *A document can be verified by anyone holding its digest* already states it | An account wall in front of the vault's own proof |
| Q32 | The lead case books and a sibling needs no visit of its own. Does a sibling case's own page still offer a booking picker? | No; a case whose collector holds a live booking on another of their cases reads that visit, its shop and its slot, and offers no picker of its own | A picker on every case, which books a second visit for items already coming in on the first |
| Q33 | Moving a booked visit: may the collector pick a different shop, or only another day and time at the shop already held? | Any shop and slot the picker offers; a move opens the same picker a first booking is taken from | The held shop's days and times alone, which makes a collector cancel and rebook to reach another shop |
