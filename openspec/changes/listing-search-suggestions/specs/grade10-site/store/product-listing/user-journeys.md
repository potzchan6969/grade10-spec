## User journeys

### grade10-site-store-product-listing-US-10: Collector finds a card by typing in search

**As a** collector,
**I want** the listing search field to suggest products and filters as I
type, and to commit my words as a chip when I submit them,
**so that** I can jump to a known card or narrow the shop without waiting on
a blind query.

**Accepted by:**

- `grade10-site-store-product-listing-SC-31` — Typing offers product and filter suggestions
- `grade10-site-store-product-listing-SC-32` — Enter commits free text as a chip
- `grade10-site-store-product-listing-SC-33` — A product suggestion opens the product
- `grade10-site-store-product-listing-SC-34` — A filter suggestion applies the facet
- `grade10-site-store-product-listing-SC-35` — Dismissing the search chip clears free text
- `grade10-site-store-product-listing-SC-36` — No suggestions still allows commit
- `grade10-site-store-product-listing-SC-37` — Suggestions stay on the store catalogue
- `grade10-site-store-product-listing-SC-38` — Waiting for suggestions shows searching
