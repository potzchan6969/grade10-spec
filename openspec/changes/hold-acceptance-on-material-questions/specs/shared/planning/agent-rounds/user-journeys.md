## ADDED User journeys

### shared-planning-agent-rounds-US-20: Product manager accepts on the questions that matter

**As a** product manager accepting a plan,
**I want** the raised questions a recommendation settles closed with their answer and its source, and only the ones that move scope or cannot be undone left for me,
**so that** I answer a few questions rather than thirty, and reopen any closed one with one reply.

### shared-planning-agent-rounds-US-21: Designer finds their asks under their name

**As a** designer,
**I want** each ask about a look to wait on me in a change for its surface, with the interim the feature ships,
**so that** I answer it from Pending in my own time and no feature waits on me.

### shared-planning-agent-rounds-US-22: Engineer builds the interim look

**As an** engineer building a feature,
**I want** the design to name the interim look the feature ships while the designer's ask is open,
**so that** I build now and a different answer reaches me as its own change.
