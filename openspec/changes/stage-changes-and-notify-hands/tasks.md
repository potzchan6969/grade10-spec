Each group's test task is therefore written first and ticked last. Groups 1, 2 and 4 have no screen: their demonstration is their tests. Group 8 is the walk of the surfaces groups 3 and 5 build, and runs after them. Group 7 is the application repository's, kept here so both boards read it.
