# Tasks

Every group lands its tests in their own commit before its code, and its readers run before its landing summary, as [Agent Rounds · The Walk](../../../docs/prds/products/shared/planning/agent-rounds.md#the-walk) asks. Each group's test task is therefore written first and ticked last. Groups 1, 2 and 4 have no screen: their demonstration is their tests. Group 8 is the walk of the surfaces groups 3 and 5 build, and runs after them. Group 7 is the application repository's, kept here so both boards read it.
