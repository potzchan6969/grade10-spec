## Goals

- A member spends any coupon they hold on the sale in front of them, whatever
  checkout or counter sale they left open.
- A counter sale nobody paid gives its coupon back an hour after its last plan,
  not the next day.
- A paid sale spends a coupon only where the shop gave its cut, and a sale that
  collected a code this store had deactivated is reported with the order on it.
- An operator's reversal is refused only while a sale claims the coupon.

## Non-Goals

- Stopping a counter cart that already carries the code from collecting.
- A new record for the claim: it stays on the coupon, and nothing changes in
  the schema.
- Telling the POS extension the moment a coupon leaves a sale. Staff see it on
  the sale's next plan.
- A counter sale that names no allocations. It is still an open question on
  the Discounts page, and this change does not answer it.

## Decisions

| Q | Asked | Decided | Instead of |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Is a coupon held for a sale nobody has paid? | No. It stays spendable until a paid order takes it, and a claim takes it off every earlier sale first. This is the Discounts page's `Nothing is held` row | Holding it for the sale that claimed it until a day's clock released it. That locked the member out of their own coupon, and it freed the coupon while its code could still be collected |
| Q2 | Is a counter sale cancelled when its coupon is claimed elsewhere? | No. The newer promise retires it: it keeps its cart, whatever reward it holds comes off, and that reward's code is deactivated; Q14 says how it settles if paid anyway. This is the Discounts page's `A retired counter sale` row, and the programme requirement `A coupon is held by nothing, and the newest claim is the only live one` | Cancelling the counter sale. The shop owns that cart and it can still collect |
| Q3 | Does a code the shop will not deactivate refuse a claim? | No. The deactivation is retried until the shop agrees, and it never refuses a claim. The shop honours a code a cart already carries whatever happens to it, so settlement guards the money. Carried by the Coupons page's `Deactivating a code` row and the programme requirement `A coupon is held by nothing, and the newest claim is the only live one` | Refusing the claim until the shop confirms. That costs the member their coupon during an outage and saves no money |
| Q4 | Which claims are still refused? | Only one carried by an online checkout the provider reports collected, or one the provider will not close. The member is told an earlier sale stands, never that the coupon is unavailable. Carried by the Coupons page's `An online checkout that took the money keeps it` line and its `An earlier sale stands` refusal, and the programme requirement `A coupon is held by nothing, and the newest claim is the only live one` | Refusing the coupon as unavailable, the sentence this change removes |
| Q5 | Is the member shown that a sale claims a coupon? | No. The wallet, the cart drawer and the till panel read every coupon as spendable, and no surface names the sale. Carried by the Coupons page's `What the member is shown` row and the programme requirement `A coupon is held by nothing, and the newest claim is the only live one` | A claimed state on the coupon. It tells the member about a sale they already left |
| Q6 | When does an unpaid sale give its coupon back? | A counter sale gives it back when another sale claims it, when a newer promise retires the sale, or an hour after its last plan. An online order gives it back when it is cancelled. An online order that only expires keeps its claim until its code can no longer be collected, and the programme's clock then releases it; a claim elsewhere tries to close it first, as for any earlier sale. Carried by the Coupons page's `A sale that ends gives it back` line and `An online order that expires` row, and the programme requirement `A coupon is held by nothing, and the newest claim is the only live one` - decided by the round, from `services/reconcile/reconcile.ts:141-152`, `:319-327` and `packages/loyalty/backend/src/services/rewards/coupons.ts:114`, `:551-575` | Every sale that expires giving its coupon back. An online order expires only when the store could not close its checkout, so it can still collect |
| Q7 | Can a counter sale take a reward again after a reward's code has left it? | No, neither the same coupon nor another one. The remedy is a new sale, and points still go on that sale. A sale a newer promise retired is closed instead and takes no new plan, as the Discounts page's `A retired counter sale keeps its cart` line says; the till tells staff to ring the goods on a new sale, never to scan the card again, because a fresh scan leaves the deactivated code on the same cart - decided by the round, from `services/pos/sale/sale.ts:137-142` and `integrations/shopify-pos/grade10/src/till/sentences.ts:45` | Minting the code again, which gives the same dead string; refusing every new plan of the sale, which blocks points after Remove every discount |
| Q8 | How does settlement tell that a counter sale carried a gift? | By the gift's line. A gift carries no code at the till: the extension discounts its line to nothing, and a code would ask the shop for the same benefit twice and use the order's one code slot. The Discounts page's `Rewards` kinds and `Reward coupon transport` row, the Coupons page's `Applying one` table, the programme requirement `A redemption settles as a coupon, whatever the reward` and the discounts requirement `A coupon reaches the order as its own ephemeral Shopify Discount code` are corrected to the implementation, `services/pos/sale/sale.ts:768-780` - decided by the round | The page's gift code at the till, which the implementation never mints |
| Q9 | Does a claim's retry key answer after the claim is given back? | Only while the claim stands. Once it is given back, the same sale asking again gets a new claim, and a claim the shop already collected still refuses a second - decided by the round, from `packages/loyalty/backend/src/services/rewards/coupons.ts:239-283`. Carried by the programme requirement `Every recorded mutation answers the same way when retried` | Answering the key forever. That refuses a coupon the member can see in their wallet |
| Q10 | What shows that this works? | Coupon claims refused as unavailable fall to zero. Coupons spent within a day of a counter sale the member walked away from are counted. Carried by the Coupons page's Product decisions measures, read as the tech design's `Measures` section names | Counting every refused claim. A claim refused because a sale took money is correct, and would hide the refusal this change removes |
| Q11 | Which sale asks for a claim again under the key of a claim that was given back? | A till sale re-planned after a plan that was refused once it had claimed: the sale keeps its order id as the key, and the refused plan gave the claim back. The next plan claims anew - decided by the round, from `services/orders/promise.ts:246`, `:423`, `:568` and `services/pos/sale/sale.ts:199`. Carried by the programme requirement `Every recorded mutation answers the same way when retried` | Leaving the rule with no route, which keeps a refused plan from ever claiming the coupon again |
| Q12 | How long does a minted code stay live, and how long does the stale-claim sweep wait? | A reward's code lives 24 hours from its order's creation; the sweep waits 25 hours from the claim, which is never earlier, so the sweep never frees a coupon whose code is live - decided by the round, from `services/coupons/mint.ts:13-27` and `packages/loyalty/backend/src/services/rewards/coupons.ts:114`. Carried by the Coupons page's `Giving a claim back` block and the programme requirement `Reversing a redemption restores the points it consumed, while its coupon is unused` | Both clocks running a day from different moments, which freed the coupon while its code could still be collected |
| Q13 | Does a sale that has taken the money but not yet settled refuse a new claim? | An online checkout the provider reports collected refuses the claim by name, and the wallet still reads the coupon as spendable, as Q5 decides. A counter sale is not known to be paid until its order arrives, so the claim moves the coupon, and the paid sale is settled against what it carried - decided by the round, from `services/orders/retire.ts:130-192`. Carried by the Coupons page's `An online checkout that took the money keeps it` line and the programme requirement `A coupon is held by nothing, and the newest claim is the only live one` | Refusing every claim on a counter sale in case it was tendered, which locks the coupon to every sale the member walks away from |
| Q14 | Does a counter sale tendered after its coupon left, with no new plan, collect at full price? | No. A cart still carrying the code collects with the cut, because the shop honours it. The sale spends the coupon where no other sale claims it, spends nothing where one does, and is reported either way. The anchor, the Discounts page and SC-19 drop "at full price" - decided by the round, from the Coupons page's `Walked away` case and `services/coupons/settle.ts:87-89` | Reporting only, which leaves the member both the cut and the coupon |
| Q15 | What leaves a counter sale when the gift reward on it is claimed elsewhere? | The claim. The gift's line stays on the cart, because the sale takes no new plan, and a sale paid showing it is settled by that line as a code is - decided by the round, from `services/coupons/settle.ts:87-97` | Naming only the cut and the code, which a gift does not have |
| Q16 | Who is told when a sale collects a deactivated code, and where do they read it? | An operator, from the commerce monitors: `store.coupons.reward_double_ride` alerts on any, and its log line names the order - decided by the round, from `services/orders/transitions.ts:397-404`. Carried by the Coupons page's `A code collected after it was let go` line and the discounts requirement `A live cart price previews locally, and mints nothing until an order claims the coupon` | A dashboard count nobody is asked to read |
| Q17 | Does an online checkout carrying neither points nor a coupon retire a counter sale that holds a reward? | Yes. Every checkout of a signed-in member, and every plan at a counter, retires the member's earlier sales carrying points or a code, whatever the newer one carries. Carried by the Discounts page's `The newer promise retires the older` line and SC-206 - decided by the round, from `services/orders/promise.ts:218-225` and `repositories/orders.ts:362-383` | Retiring only where the newer sale carries points or a coupon, which leaves a reward locked to a counter sale the member walked away from |
| Q18 | When a counter sale runs out its hour, does a gift's line stay on the cart? | Yes. Nothing of the store runs at the counter once staff walk away, so the claim leaves the sale and the gift's line stays on the shop's cart. A sale paid showing it is settled by that line, as a code is. Carried by the Discounts page's `Walked away with a reward` line and SC-25 - decided by the round, from `services/reconcile/reconcile.ts:145-164` and `services/coupons/rides.ts:126-129` | Removing the line at the hour, which needs the till open and staff at it |
| Q19 | How does a counter sale that ran out its hour refuse its next plan, when its till session lives ten minutes? | Through the fresh scan. A session lives ten minutes from its scan and a sale's hour runs from its last plan, so the next plan on that cart always comes from a new session. A plan from a new session on a cart still carrying another sale's reward code is refused as a closed sale, and the till asks for a new sale. Carried by the Discounts page's `A row that closed cannot be rewritten` line and the discounts requirement `The same discount-code mechanism applies online and at the POS till` - decided by the round, from `pos/deps.ts:54`, `services/pos/identity/sessions.ts:141` and `integrations/shopify-pos/grade10/src/till/sentences.ts:32` | Refusing only the closed sale's own next plan, which its expired session never sends; the fresh scan the till then asks for would mint a second code beside the dead one |
