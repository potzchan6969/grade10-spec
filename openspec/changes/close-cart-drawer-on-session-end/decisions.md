## Goals

- An open cart drawer closes by itself when the member it was open for is signed out, in another tab or by expiry.
- The close says nothing: no toast and no notice.
- A guest's drawer, and a drawer open across a switch from one member to another, stay open.

## Non-Goals

- Emptying this browser's cart on sign-out. That stays with `shared/auth/sign-out`.
- Closing or withdrawing anything else a tab holds open when its session ends. The pages that need a session keep asking for one, as `shared/auth/session` states.
- Opening the cart again when a collector signs in after their session ended, through the header or the dialog checkout opens. A sign-in started from the drawer's own Sign in link still reopens it (Q11).
- Changing what the drawer shows while a session read is still pending (Q13).
- What checkout asks of a collector whose session has ended as they press Proceed to Checkout (Q14).
- A notice, a toast or a prompt to sign in as the drawer closes.

## Decisions

| Q | Asked | Decided | Instead of |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Should an already-open cart close silently when this site's session ends in another tab? | Close silently - decided by the product owner on #625, 2026-10-09 | Staying open and empty, as it does today: the drawer reads as a cart with nothing in it, beside a header that says Sign In |
| Q2 | Does a guest's drawer close when the session reads as signed out? | No. Only a member's session ending closes it; a guest never had a session to lose - decided by the round (recommended) | Closing on every signed-out reading, which would shut a guest's drawer the moment they open it, against the durable rule that a signed-out Cart press opens the same drawer |
| Q3 | Does a session still being read close the drawer? | No. A reading in flight decides nobody, so a member whose session is still good never sees the drawer shut - decided by the round (recommended) | Closing on the first reading that is not a member, which would shut the drawer during a routine re-read |
| Q4 | Does a switch from one member to another close the drawer? | No. The drawer shows the new member's cart, as `shared/auth/session` already asks of every tab - decided by the round (recommended) | Closing on any change of person, which discards what the new member's own cart is showing and is not what the product owner asked |
| Q5 | When checkout meets an expired session, does the drawer stay open? | No. It closes like any other end of a member's session, and signing back in does not reopen it. What checkout asks of the collector next is `grade10-site/store/checkout`'s, not this change's (Q14) - decided by the round (recommended), confirmed by @seankcw, 2026-10-16 | Reopening the drawer on sign-in from that path, which keeps the member at Proceed to Checkout but makes one path the exception to a rule the product owner stated without one |
| Q6 | How soon must the drawer close once the session has ended? | When the tab shows the person signed out, which is when its header shows Sign In. `shared/auth/session` has a tab keep up promptly with a sign-out in another tab of this browser, and no later than the person's return in every other case, such as expiry. The drawer is never open beside that header - decided by the round (recommended) | Closing later than the header, or only on a reload, which leaves the empty drawer beside Sign In, the symptom in #625 |
| Q7 | A person switches member by signing out and signing in in the other tab. Does the drawer close if this tab reads the signed-out step between? | It follows what the tab reads. It closes on any reading that shows nobody signed in, and stays open when the tab reads one member and then another with no such reading between. The same member signing out and in follows the same rule. A person who switches member by signing out and in elsewhere finds the drawer closed, because the open tab keeps up promptly (Q6) and reads nobody between - decided by the round (recommended) | Keeping the drawer open across a signed-out reading, which leaves it open for a session this tab saw end |
| Q8 | Does a session read that fails, with the service unreachable or an error, close the drawer? | No. A failed read is not the end of a session: `shared/auth/session` has a tab that cannot read the session keep what it showed - decided by the round (recommended) | Counting a failed read as nobody, which shuts a member's drawer when the network drops and their session is still good |
| Q9 | A guest's drawer is open and the collector signs in in another tab. Does it close? | No. Nobody's session ended. It stays open and shows the member's cart once this tab shows the member, as `shared/auth/session` asks of a tab that learns of a sign-in - decided by the round (recommended) | Closing on any change of session, which Q2 and Q4 already rule out |
| Q10 | What happens to a cart write, a checkout creation or a read already sent when the session ends? | The drawer closes like any other close. The request already sent ends as it would, and its late answer changes nothing in a drawer opened afterwards - decided by the round (recommended) | Cancelling work already sent, which the drawer cannot do, or letting a late answer reach the next collector's drawer |
| Q11 | Does a sign-in started from the guest drawer's Sign in link still reopen the drawer after a session end closed an earlier one? | Yes. That drawer was opened afterwards, and the Cart page and `add-store-guest-checkout` already say its sign-in reopens it with points and the wallet. Only a sign-in the collector does not start from an open drawer leaves it closed - decided by the round (recommended) | Dropping the reopen after any session end, which breaks the guest's sign-in path to points and the wallet |
| Q12 | Does the close hold on every surface the drawer opens over? | Yes, on every one, since the drawer is the same everywhere. A surface that itself needs a session makes its own sign-in ask, as `shared/auth/session` states - decided by the round (recommended) | Closing only on Store surfaces, which leaves an empty drawer open over an Auction page after a sign-out |
| Q13 | Does this change alter what the drawer shows while a member's session is read again? | No. It adds only the close. The cart scope keeps reading a session still being read as a guest's, as it does today - decided by the round (recommended) | Keeping the member's cart through the read, which changes the cart scope outside this change |
| Q14 | When a tab whose session has ended presses Proceed to Checkout, does the collector meet a sign-in dialog or check out as a guest? | This change does not decide it. The drawer closes, and what checkout asks next stays with `grade10-site/store/checkout`. Its page says a collector with no session checks out as a guest, and the in-flight `add-shopify-checkout-integration` has a signed-out collector asked to sign in; the question is a ❓ on the Checkout page for its owner - decided by the round (recommended) | Writing the sign-in dialog into this requirement, which would restate a rule another capability owns and may contradict its guest checkout |

## Raised

| Capability | Raised | Landed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| grade10-site/store/cart-drawer | **How soon the drawer closes.** The journey says the drawer is gone when the collector comes back to the tab; the product page says it closes by itself. Must it also close while the tab stays in view and untouched when the session ends, or only when the tab next reads the session? Is it never left open beside a header that already says Sign In? The cases use a return to the tab as the trigger and expect no drawer beside Sign In. | Q6 |
| grade10-site/store/cart-drawer | **A sign-out followed by a sign-in in the other tab.** Q4 keeps the drawer open across a switch from one member to another. A person makes that switch by signing out and signing in as the other member, so the open tab may read the signed-out state between the two. If it does, does the drawer close and stay closed, or is only a switch with no signed-out reading between kept open? Does the same member signing out and back in count the same way? The switch case signs out then in and expects the drawer to stay open. | Q7 |
| grade10-site/store/cart-drawer | **A session read that fails.** Q3 settles a read still in flight. When the read ends in an error, such as a network failure or a server error, instead of an answer, does the drawer stay open like a read in flight, or does the failure count as not a member? No case is written. | Q8 |
| grade10-site/store/cart-drawer | **A guest who becomes a member in another tab.** Q2 keeps a guest's drawer open and Q4 a switch between members. A guest's drawer open in one tab while the collector signs in in another is neither. Does it stay open on the browser's cart, move to the member's cart, or close? No case is written. | Q9 |
| grade10-site/store/cart-drawer | **Work in flight when the drawer closes.** When the session ends with a cart write (quantity, code or points) or a checkout creation already sent, or a read still unanswered, what becomes of that work, and may its late answer reach the guest drawer opened afterwards? The cases expect only that the drawer closes and a late read does not bring it back. | Q10 |
| grade10-site/store/cart-drawer | **Signing in from the guest drawer after the close.** The cart page says signing in from the signed-out drawer's Sign in link reopens the drawer with points and the wallet; Q5 and the non-goals say signing back in does not reopen a drawer a session end closed. Does the first rule still hold when the guest drawer was opened after a member's session ended, so that "does not reopen" covers only a sign-in that did not start from an open drawer? The cases sign in through the header and the checkout dialog only. | Q11 |
| grade10-site/store/cart-drawer | **Which surfaces the close covers.** The cart page puts Cart in the header of every surface, Auction included. Does the close hold on every surface the drawer opens over, including one that itself needs a session, or only on Store surfaces? The cases open the drawer over a Store surface only. | Q12 |
| grade10-site/store/cart-drawer | **When an open tab learns of a sign-out.** Q6 says a tab learns of a sign-out when the person returns to it. `shared/auth/session` has a tab keep up promptly with a session that changed in another tab of the same browser, and on return only in every other case, such as expiry. Which timing governs the drawer's close? It decides the switch route: if the open tab learns of the sign-out at once, it reads nobody before the other member signs in and Q7 closes the drawer; if it learns on return, it reads the new member directly and the drawer stays open. The switch case signs out and then in as the other member in the second tab, with the first tab out of view, and expects the drawer still open. | Q6 |
| grade10-site/store/cart-drawer | **A sign-in dialog at an expired checkout.** Q5 and the requirement have Proceed to Checkout, on a session that has ended, open a sign-in dialog that checkout asks for. The current Checkout page and spec have a collector with no session check out as a guest and never be asked to sign in; only the older `add-shopify-checkout-integration` delta says a signed-out collector is asked to sign in. When a tab whose session has ended presses Proceed to Checkout, does the collector meet a sign-in dialog, or does the drawer close and the collector, now a guest, check out as a guest? The expired-checkout case expects the dialog and a closed drawer. | Q14 |
