## User journeys

### grade10-site-store-shopify-commerce-US-06: Shopper meets the identity bar at checkout

**As a** shopper buying goods worth the bar or more,
**I want** to be told before paying that a verified identity is needed, and where to get one,
**so that** I am not charged for an order the store cannot complete, and I know what to do next.

**Accepted by:**

- `grade10-site-store-shopify-commerce-SC-26` — An unverified buyer above the bar is sent to verify
- `grade10-site-store-shopify-commerce-SC-27` — A verified buyer above the bar checks out
- `grade10-site-store-shopify-commerce-SC-28` — A basket below the bar asks nothing
- `grade10-site-store-shopify-commerce-SC-29` — A guest above the bar is asked to sign in
