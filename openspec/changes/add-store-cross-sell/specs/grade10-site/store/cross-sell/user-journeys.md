## ADDED User journeys

### grade10-site-store-cross-sell-US-01: Collector opens a card the stock keeper chose for this one

**As a** collector reading a card,
**I want** the cards the stock keeper chose to go with it under it,
**so that** I can open the next card worth having without going back to the
listing.

### grade10-site-store-cross-sell-US-02: Collector opens a similar card where the shop chose none

**As a** collector reading a card nobody chose picks for,
**I want** cards like it under it, by its world, its language and its type,
**so that** a card with no picks still leads me somewhere.

### grade10-site-store-cross-sell-US-03: Collector reads a card with nothing to show beside it

**As a** collector reading a card with no picks and nothing similar,
**I want** the page whole, with no rail and no empty space,
**so that** the page does not look broken where there is simply nothing to
show.

### grade10-site-store-cross-sell-US-04: Stock keeper chooses the cards shown with a card

**As a** stock keeper,
**I want** to choose the cards shown with a card in the Shopify dashboard, on
the card itself, and see them on its page within the page's own minute,
**so that** what I know belongs together is what the collector sees.
