## Goals

- Shoppers can inspect supplied promo and points context without encountering actions the consumer cannot perform.
- Tender actions remain available when the consumer supplies the callback that can perform them.

## Non-Goals

- Changing callback types, adding props, or changing the Grade10 cart host and its wiring.
- Applying tender, calculating quotes, changing cart totals, or creating checkout sessions.
- Changing backend behavior, persistence, checkout APIs, design tokens, or Figma files.
