## Goals

- The tier card states standing; navigation lives in the rail.
- Removing an affordance is an export contract change, recorded as one.

## Non-Goals

- The rest of the card — skin, balance, worth, qualifying progress, earn
  line — stays as it is.
- The Tiers & Benefits surface itself; only the same-page link to it goes.

## Settled

- The affordance is removed, not made optional — keeping `href`/`onClick`
  props would keep an interactive affordance the design no longer carries.
