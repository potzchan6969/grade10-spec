## ADDED User journeys

### grade10-site-consignment-consignor-portal-US-01: Consignor checks a consignment's status

**As a** signed-in consignor,
**I want** to see each of my consignments with every item's status - one
bound for auction showing before its lot lists - its channel, its agreed
price, the commission rate and what I'd net, its sale and its payout,
**so that** I never have to ask the shop where my items are.

### grade10-site-consignment-consignor-portal-US-02: Consignor reads their paper

**As a** signed-in consignor,
**I want** my agreement, receipts, statements and invoices and the emails
the consignment sent,
**so that** I hold the same record the shop does.

### grade10-site-consignment-consignor-portal-US-03: Consignor's account is linked at intake

**As a** consignor with a Grade10 account,
**I want** it linked when I consign,
**so that** my consignments appear on my signed-in page.
