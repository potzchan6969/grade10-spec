## ADDED User journeys

### grade10-site-consignment-consignor-notifications-US-01: Consignor hears each thing that happens

**As a** collector who consigns,
**I want** an email for each thing that happens to my consignment -
received, signed, on sale, repriced, sold, payout set, paid, ending,
returned - in my language,
**so that** I always know where it stands without asking the shop.

### grade10-site-consignment-consignor-notifications-US-02: Staff resend a message that never went

**As a** member of shop staff,
**I want** a consignment flagged when a message to its consignor ran out of
attempts, and to send it again,
**so that** a provider outage costs a delay rather than a consignor never
told.
