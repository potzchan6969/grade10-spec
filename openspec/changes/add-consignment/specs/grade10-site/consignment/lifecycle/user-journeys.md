## ADDED User journeys

### grade10-site-consignment-lifecycle-US-01: Consignor hands collectables in at the counter

**As a** collector who consigns,
**I want** to bring my graded collectables to the shop, agree a price and
terms with staff and sign the agreement,
**so that** the house sells them for me for a commission.

### grade10-site-consignment-lifecycle-US-02: Consignor hands collectables in at an event

**As a** collector who consigns at an event such as a card show,
**I want** staff to take my items in there and record where the consignment
was taken,
**so that** I can consign without travelling to the shop.

### grade10-site-consignment-lifecycle-US-03: Staff register the consignment and agree the terms

**As a** member of shop staff,
**I want** to record the channel - the store or the auction - register each
item's market value and the consignment rates, agree a list price and floor
and link the consignor's Grade10 account,
**so that** the consignment is ready to sign in one visit.

### grade10-site-consignment-lifecycle-US-04: Consignor's item goes on sale in the recorded channel

**As a** collector who consigns,
**I want** each item logged in Shopify once the consignment form is
completed and to become one store product, or a draft lot staff place at
auction, in the channel we agreed,
**so that** it is on sale without me listing anything.

### grade10-site-consignment-lifecycle-US-05: Consignor is told when the price moves

**As a** collector who consigns,
**I want** staff to move the price within the floor and hear when they do,
with anything below it needing my written yes,
**so that** the price can respond without my item going cheap.

### grade10-site-consignment-lifecycle-US-06: Consignor picks what an unsold item does next

**As a** collector who consigns,
**I want** to pick what happens when the term ends or the lot goes unsold,
and to take an item back early,
**so that** my item is never stranded.

### grade10-site-consignment-lifecycle-US-07: Consignor follows a return home

**As a** collector whose consigned item is coming back,
**I want** it to travel under its tracking,
**so that** I can follow it until it is back with me.

### grade10-site-consignment-lifecycle-US-08: A company consigns under its own name

**As a** person representing a company,
**I want** to consign under the company with its own facts - its registered
office address among them,
**so that** the consignment and the payout are the company's.
