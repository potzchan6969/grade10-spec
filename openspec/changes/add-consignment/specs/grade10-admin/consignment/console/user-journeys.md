## ADDED User journeys

### grade10-admin-consignment-console-US-01: Staff work the queue by what waits

**As a** member of shop staff,
**I want** the consignment queue cut by what each one waits for - new, to
price, to sign, to list, on sale, sold, payout due, ended, returning -
with a badge naming why a row needs me,
**so that** I know what to pick up next without being emailed anything.

### grade10-admin-consignment-console-US-02: Staff run one consignment's acts

**As a** member of shop staff,
**I want** one consignment's page to offer each act with its rule stated
first - the floor, the schedule, the term - and a withheld act to say why
in words,
**so that** I act inside the rules without memorising them.

### grade10-admin-consignment-console-US-03: Staff receive a consignment at intake

**As a** member of shop staff,
**I want** the receive act to record where the consignment was taken and
its channel - the store or the auction - and to register each item's
market value and the consignment rates,
**so that** the commercial facts are captured once at the start.

### grade10-admin-consignment-console-US-04: Staff clear a flag on a consignment out of step

**As a** member of shop staff,
**I want** a flag raised on anything out of step - an item sold twice, a
sale the channel reported that the consignment cannot take, a price that
moved, an amount that differs - and to dismiss it once dealt with,
**so that** nothing drifts quietly between the consignment and its
channel.

### grade10-admin-consignment-console-US-05: Treasurer works the payout due list

**As a** treasurer,
**I want** every payout past its window listed oldest first with what it
is made of, to record the payout and export the period,
**so that** consignors are paid on their due day.

### grade10-admin-consignment-console-US-06: Admin changes a setting

**As an** admin,
**I want** every figure - commission by channel and price band, the
incentive, the minimum, fees, the term, the windows, identity, when a
consignor is paid - to be a setting I change, pinned to the next agreement
prepared,
**so that** terms move without touching consignments already signed or
waiting.

### grade10-admin-consignment-console-US-07: Admin names a consignor where they agreed

**As an** admin,
**I want** to name a consignor on the store and the lot only where they
agreed on the agreement, under the display name they gave,
**so that** a consenting seller can be shown and nobody is named by
mistake.
