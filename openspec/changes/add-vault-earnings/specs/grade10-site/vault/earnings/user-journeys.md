## ADDED User journeys

### g10.vault-earnings.US1: Owner watches each vaulted card earn

**As an** owner of cards in the vault,
**I want** each of my cards to earn its own incentive every day from the day its contract takes effect,
**so that** keeping cards with Grade10 pays me while I hold them.

### g10.vault-earnings.US2: Owner reads what can be cashed out and what is still locked

**As an** owner of cards in the vault,
**I want** one balance that shows what I can cash out and what is locked until which day,
**so that** I know what I have earned and when it becomes mine to take.

### g10.vault-earnings.US3: Owner pays the fee to take a card back early

**As an** owner taking a card back before its early-withdrawal period ends,
**I want** to see the fee first and pay it online or at the shop's till,
**so that** I can collect the card without settling the money by hand.

### g10.vault-earnings.US4: Operator takes the fee at the till against the withdrawal

**As a** shop operator,
**I want** to ring up an owner's early withdrawal fee at the till against the withdrawal's reference,
**so that** the payment is tied to that withdrawal and the card can be released.

### g10.vault-earnings.US5: Operator pauses a card or an account from earning

**As a** shop operator,
**I want** to stop a card, or every card on an account, from earning until I release it,
**so that** a card under question costs nothing while it is looked into.
