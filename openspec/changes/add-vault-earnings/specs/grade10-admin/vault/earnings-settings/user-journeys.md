## ADDED User journeys

### g10adm.vault-earnings-settings.US1: Admin changes an earnings figure without a release

**As an** admin,
**I want** to change the rate, the lock and early-withdrawal periods, the fee and the price rules in the console,
**so that** the product can follow the owner's evolving draft without waiting on engineering.

### g10adm.vault-earnings-settings.US2: Admin picks how the locked days are credited

**As an** admin,
**I want** to switch between crediting the first days daily and locked, or holding them until the lock ends,
**so that** the owner's open decision is a setting rather than a rebuild.

### g10adm.vault-earnings-settings.US3: Admin switches when identity is asked for

**As an** admin,
**I want** to turn each point at which the vault asks for a verified identity on or off,
**so that** the KYC rule can move when the owner decides it.

### g10adm.vault-earnings-settings.US4: Auditor reads who changed a figure and what it was

**As an** auditor,
**I want** every figure change logged with who made it, when, and the value before and after,
**so that** any day's credit can be explained by the figures in force.
