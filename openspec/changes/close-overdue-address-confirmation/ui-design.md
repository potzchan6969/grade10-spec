## Winner Order

- **Setup Overdue** - Hide Confirm delivery address. Show a
  missed-deadline alert with Contact Us. Keep the order's normal shell and
  progress presentation.
- **Reopened** - Show Confirm delivery address and the fresh absolute deadline.
- **Recorded by operator** - Show the locked confirmed address. Do not restore
  winner address controls.

## Post-Sale

- **Overdue address** - Show Reopen address form and Record address controls
  to payment-processing operators. The Reopen dialog requires a reason.
- **Refusals** - Disable and explain both controls after invoice send. A user
  without the grant sees disabled controls; the server refuses the action.
- **Audit** - Show the named operator, time and reason for every reopen or
  operator-recorded address in the existing invoice log.
