## Goals

- An inventory admin sees every unit the product holds in Cert ID details, numbered or not
- An inventory admin corrects a wrong Cert ID on an available unit without removing it
- An inventory admin gives an available unit of regular stock a Cert ID
- The history shows who changed a Cert ID, when, and from what to what

## Non-Goals

- Giving each unit of regular stock its own record or its own history before it has a Cert ID
- Listing sold or withdrawn regular stock, which no unit record tracks
- Changing the Cert ID of a reserved, sold, withdrawn or vaulted unit
- Clearing a Cert ID to turn a Cert record back into regular stock
- Editing Grade Issuer, Grade, Autograph Grade or Serial on an existing Cert record
- Assigning or changing Cert IDs in bulk, or through the inventory workbook import
- Rewriting the copy of an ended Auction listing, or telling collectors about a change

| Q4  | Can a Cert ID be cleared?                                       | No: it can be changed to another non-empty Cert ID; a wrong record is removed with Remove physical unit - @mason5991, 2026-10-02                                                                                       | Clearing it back to regular stock, which drops its copy facts and leaves its tagged media with nothing to point at                                                                                                 |
| Q5  | Does a Cert ID change need a reason?                            | Optional remarks, as at intake - @mason5991, 2026-10-02                                                                                                                                                                | A required reason, as withdraw and Remove physical unit ask; the history already names the actor and both Cert IDs                                                                                                 |
| Q6  | Which units can change?                                         | An available unit only: a Cert record whose status is available with no active hold, or one available unit of regular stock outside every hold; the others show why the action is not offered - decided by the round   | A reserved unit too, which would move the Cert ID under a live listing a collector is bidding on                                                                                                                   |
| Q7  | What does a changed Cert ID keep?                               | The same record, so its status, copy facts, tagged media and earlier history stay with it; the new Cert ID is checked unique within the product after trimming - decided by the round                                  | Removing the record and creating a new one, which splits one unit's history in two and drops its tagged media                                                                                                      |
| Q8  | How does the history record a change?                           | One entry, `Cert ID change`, quantity one, with the Cert ID before and after (`No Cert ID` before an assignment), actor, time and remarks; it shows in the product's history and in that unit's - decided by the round | Two actions, one for a correction and one for an assignment, which the before value already tells apart                                                                                                            |
| Q9  | What does an assignment do to the counts?                       | Stock, reserved, sold and the available total stay the same; the `No Cert ID` count falls by one and the product has one more available Cert record - decided by the round                                             | Recording it as a withdrawal and an intake, which inflates both totals                                                                                                                                             |
| Q10 | What does an Auction listing show after the change?             | The unit's current Cert ID, since a listing reads its Cert record; an ended listing whose unit was released shows the corrected Cert ID - decided by the round                                                         | Freezing the Cert ID on the listing at close, which keeps a wrong number on the record of an Unsold lot                                                                                                            |
| Q11 | Who can change a Cert ID?                                       | An inventory admin who may write inventory, the same grant as intake; others see the rows and the history only - decided by the round                                                                                  | A new grant, which no team has asked for                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Q12 | How is success measured?                                        | Physical-unit removals followed by a same-product intake within a day, which should fall to none - decided by the round                                                                                                | No measure                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
