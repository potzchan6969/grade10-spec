## ADDED User journeys

### winner-order-US-13: Winner quotes their order

**As a** winner of an auction lot,
**I want** my order to have a clear, stable order ID I can reference,
**so that** I can quote it when contacting support or making inquiries about my purchase.

### winner-order-US-14: Winner reviews invoice and payment details

**As a** winner,
**I want** my invoice and payment receipts to show stable public references and a copyable payment reference,
**so that** I can contact Grade10, make a payment, and reconcile charges without exposing internal system keys.
