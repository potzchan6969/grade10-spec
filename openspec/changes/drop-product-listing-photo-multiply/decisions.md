## Goals

- The listing photo is drawn as supplied over the well. It is not multiplied
  onto the well gradient.

## Non-Goals

- Photo fit and crop — `fit-product-listing-photo`.
- Phone cart visibility and sort defaults.

## Decisions

| Q | Asked | Decided | Instead of |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Is the photo multiplied onto the well? | No. The image draws as supplied. Multiply muddies a letterboxed catalogue photo - decided by the round | Multiplying against the well so white studio fills read as transparent |
