## User journeys

### grade10-site-site-page-shell-US-06: Collector opens their cart from the header

**As a** collector on a Store surface,
**I want** the Cart control to take me to my own cart, signing me in first when
I am not,
**so that** the cart I open is the one holding what I picked, rather than an
empty room.

**Accepted by:**

- `grade10-site-site-page-shell-SC-21` — A signed-out collector presses Cart
- `grade10-site-site-page-shell-SC-22` — Sign-in opens the cart they asked for
- `grade10-site-site-page-shell-SC-23` — Dismissing sign-in opens nothing
- `grade10-site-site-page-shell-SC-24` — A member presses Cart
