## ADDED User journeys

### grade10-site-grading-dropoff-booking-US-01: Collector books the drop-off from the plan

**As a** collector who has reviewed the plan,
**I want** to pick the shop, a day in the next 30 days and a time in the shop's own zone, seeing beside the day the batch it makes and the Friday the cards would leave,
**so that** I arrive at a desk expecting my list and know which batch my cards join.

### grade10-site-grading-dropoff-booking-US-02: Collector moves or cancels the drop-off

**As a** collector who cannot make the visit,
**I want** to move or cancel it from the submission page any time before it starts and to be emailed either way,
**so that** a change of plans costs me a minute and not the list.

### grade10-site-grading-dropoff-booking-US-03: Collector who missed the drop-off books another

**As a** collector who missed the visit,
**I want** the visit to close and the list and the estimate to stay exactly as they were, with a line on the page to book again,
**so that** one missed day does not cost me the plan.

### grade10-site-grading-dropoff-booking-US-04: Collector brings a second submission to the same drop-off

**As a** collector whose cards need two levels,
**I want** the second submission to join the drop-off already booked, one visit and both lists,
**so that** I make one trip to the shop.

### grade10-site-grading-dropoff-booking-US-05: Walk-in books a drop-off without a list

**As a** collector who does not want to list cards on my phone,
**I want** to book the Grading visit with a name and an email and have the cards listed with me at the counter,
**so that** I can still hand cards in on a booked slot.

### grade10-site-grading-dropoff-booking-US-06: Collector knows what to bring and what happens at the counter

**As a** collector with a drop-off booked,
**I want** the booked page and the booked email to say what to bring, that staff check each card against the list, that I sign and then pay, and the day the cards leave with the estimated day back,
**so that** the visit is over in one go with nothing forgotten at home.
