## ADDED User journeys

### grade10-site-grading-collector-notifications-US-01: Collector hears about everything that happens to the submission

**As a** collector,
**I want** one email for each thing that happens to my submission, in English, with a link straight to the page that needs no account,
**so that** I never have to ask the shop what stage my cards are at.

### grade10-site-grading-collector-notifications-US-02: Collector hears about the drop-off from the submission

**As a** collector with a drop-off booked,
**I want** the booked, moved, cancelled, missed and day-before messages to come from the submission with the list, the fee and the day the cards leave,
**so that** one thread tells me about the visit and the cards together.

### grade10-site-grading-collector-notifications-US-03: Collector in a batch is told it is running late

**As a** collector whose batch is past its estimate,
**I want** an email the day the shop sets the new date, with the grader's stage, the old date and the new one,
**so that** I know nothing changes at the counter and when to expect the cards.

### grade10-site-grading-collector-notifications-US-04: Collector who has not collected is reminded on the ladder

**As a** collector whose cards are ready,
**I want** a reminder at 30 and 60 days, the storage-fee email on day 90 and the written notice on day 180, each carrying the code and what is due,
**so that** every step of the ladder reaches me before it costs me anything.

### grade10-site-grading-collector-notifications-US-05: Collector is told the same day a card did not come back

**As a** collector one of whose cards was not returned or came back damaged,
**I want** an email the day the batch is received saying what is settled, what is refunded and by when, with the other cards ready now,
**so that** the bad news comes from the shop before I find out at the counter.

### grade10-site-grading-collector-notifications-US-06: Collector is not emailed about what the counter already told them

**As a** collector who was refused a card at the counter or who named someone to collect on the page,
**I want** no email about it, the receipt and History carrying it instead,
**so that** my inbox holds only what I could not already see.

### grade10-site-grading-collector-notifications-US-07: Operator picks up a message that never went

**As a** member of shop staff,
**I want** a submission to be flagged when a message to its collector ran out of attempts, and to be able to send it again,
**so that** a provider outage costs a delay rather than a collector who was never told.
