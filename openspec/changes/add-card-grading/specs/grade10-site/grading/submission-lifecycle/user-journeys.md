## ADDED User journeys

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-01: Collector follows the submission from booked to home on one page

**As a** collector with cards in the shop's hands,
**I want** the page to read one status in my words, a chip saying whose move it is, and a rail from Planned to Home,
**so that** I never have to ask the shop where my cards are.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-02: Collector reads the grader's stages while the cards are away

**As a** collector whose batch has left,
**I want** the page to show the grader's stages in its own words, the estimate, and Running late with the new date once it is set,
**so that** waiting on the grader never reads as waiting on the shop.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-03: Collector withdraws a card before the batch closes

**As a** collector who changed my mind after hand-in,
**I want** to message the shop before Thursday 19:00 and collect the card at the counter against a receipt, with its fee back at the till,
**so that** a card I want to keep does not leave with the batch.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-04: Collector sees why a card was refused at the counter

**As a** collector whose card the grader would not take,
**I want** the card to stay in my hands with the reason in plain words on the page and the receipt, never charged, and the list and the estimate to drop to the cards that go on,
**so that** the rest of the submission carries on without me.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-05: Collector learns why a card came back ungraded

**As a** collector whose card came back raw,
**I want** the grader's code and note on the card and a plain line saying the fee stands,
**so that** I know what the grader found and that I was told before I booked.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-06: Collector learns the upcharge the day the grades post

**As a** collector whose card came back worth more than the level allows,
**I want** the difference named on the grades email and on the page as due at the counter before collection,
**so that** I know what to settle before I come in.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-07: Collector who does not accept a grade knows what a review is

**As a** collector unhappy with a grade,
**I want** the page to say the grade is the grader's decision and a review is a new submission at the grader's review fee,
**so that** I ask at the counter rather than argue with the shop.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-08: Collector collects the rest while the grader holds one card

**As a** collector one of whose cards the grader kept back,
**I want** the others ready to collect now, the receipt to name the card still out with the grader's expected date, and a second hand-back to close the submission,
**so that** one card does not hold the rest.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-09: Collector is settled for a card that did not come back

**As a** collector whose card was lost or damaged in the grader's hands or in transit,
**I want** to be told the same day, settled at the card's declared value with its fee refunded inside the settlement window, and offered the card back if it turns up,
**so that** I never wait on the shop's claim against the grader or the courier.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-10: Collector gets money back the way it was paid

**As a** collector owed a refund for a refused, withdrawn or unreturned card,
**I want** it back at the till the way I paid, with the page saying what came back and why,
**so that** I can see the money and the reason in one place.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-11: Collector collects with the pickup code

**As a** collector whose cards are ready,
**I want** a four-digit code on the page and in the email, the shop's hours with no booking needed, what is due, and whether to bring an ID,
**so that** I walk in and leave with the slabs on one visit.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-12: Collector names somebody else to collect

**As a** collector who cannot come in,
**I want** to name one person by their full name on the page before they come, change or remove them any time before collection, and see it logged in History,
**so that** they collect with the code and the ID the pickup card asks for, and nobody else can.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-13: Collector keeps a slab in the vault instead of taking it home

**As a** collector collecting a high grade,
**I want** to say at the counter that the slab goes into a vault case, with the identity check and the custody agreement done there and storage free,
**so that** the slab is kept safe without a second visit.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-14: Collector who leaves the cards is reminded, charged and then given notice

**As a** collector who has not collected,
**I want** a reminder at 30 and 60 days costing nothing, the storage fee from day 90 due before collection, and written notice at 180 days before anything else can happen, with the cards mine until then,
**so that** I am nudged, never surprised, and can still vault them instead.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-15: Collector keeps the graded record after collection

**As a** collector who has collected,
**I want** the grade, grader and cert per slab with a look-up link, the slab photographs and the three documents with their fingerprints to stay on the page and under my account when I keep one,
**so that** a vault valuation or an auction reads the record from there and my slab never enters the shop's stock.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-16: Collector cancels a submission before handing the cards in

**As a** collector who no longer wants the cards graded,
**I want** to cancel the submission from the page any time before the cards are handed in, with the drop-off going with it,
**so that** nothing is left open in my name and nothing is owed.

### grade10-site-grading-submission-lifecycle-US-17: Collector is offered only what the status allows

**As a** collector reading the page,
**I want** each status to offer only the acts it allows, editing the list before hand-in, withdrawing a card at Handed in, naming a collector when ready, and nothing while the grader has the cards,
**so that** I am never shown a button that will only be refused.
