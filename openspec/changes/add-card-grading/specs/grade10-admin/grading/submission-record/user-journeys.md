## ADDED User journeys

### grade10-admin-grading-submission-record-US-01: Operator reads one submission's cards after the grades

**As a** member of shop staff opening a submission,
**I want** per card the intake id, the declared value, the level and the one it was moved to, the grade and cert in the grader's words and the outcome, with the header chips naming what is due, what is ungraded and which batch it is in,
**so that** I can answer a collector on the phone from one screen.

### grade10-admin-grading-submission-record-US-02: Treasurer waives an upcharge with a second person

**As a** treasurer,
**I want** to waive the difference the grader charged with a reason and a second approve holder who is not me, once the cards are back,
**so that** nobody can write off money alone and the collector's due drops to nothing before they collect.

### grade10-admin-grading-submission-record-US-03: Treasurer records a settlement for a card that did not come back

**As a** treasurer,
**I want** to record the declared value settled and the fee refunded for a card not returned or damaged, at the till or by transfer, inside the window,
**so that** the collector is paid without waiting on the shop's claim and the money book shows what went out and why.

### grade10-admin-grading-submission-record-US-04: Operator withdraws a card the collector asked back

**As a** member of shop staff at Handed in,
**I want** to withdraw one card until the batch closes, refunding its POS line and releasing it against a hand-back receipt,
**so that** the card leaves the intake bag with a record and the rest go on.

### grade10-admin-grading-submission-record-US-05: Operator reads the money on a submission

**As a** member of shop staff,
**I want** the Money tab to read what was paid at hand-in with its POS reference, the upcharge from the invoice, what is to settle before collection and every refund,
**so that** the till and the record say the same figure.

### grade10-admin-grading-submission-record-US-06: Operator shows, copies or resends a document

**As a** member of shop staff,
**I want** the Documents tab to hold the agreement, the intake receipt and the hand-back receipt with their fingerprints, and to show one on the iPad, copy its link or send it again,
**so that** a collector who lost an email gets the same sealed copy.

### grade10-admin-grading-submission-record-US-07: Auditor reconstructs one submission's history

**As** somebody holding a submission in a dispute,
**I want** every event on the timeline with the figures it carried and the grader's stages in its words, staff-only entries kept from the collector, and every action filed under the submission on the audit chain,
**so that** the record can be tested rather than believed.

### grade10-admin-grading-submission-record-US-08: Operator sends the grades email again

**As a** member of shop staff,
**I want** to send the same grades email again from the submission, and to be refused a cancel once the cards have left,
**so that** a collector who asks is answered and no act runs where the machine forbids it.
