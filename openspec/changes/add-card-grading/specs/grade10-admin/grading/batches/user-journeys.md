## ADDED User journeys

### grade10-admin-grading-batches-US-01: Operator ships the batch that closed

**As a** member of shop staff on a Friday,
**I want** the batch closed at Thursday 19:00 with its packing list, the grader's order number, the courier and tracking, the insured total against the courier's cover and the estimate from the ship day, and one act that marks every submission in it as sent and emails every collector,
**so that** one parcel to one grader at one level leaves with one record.

### grade10-admin-grading-batches-US-02: Operator receives a batch against the grader's manifest

**As a** member of shop staff opening a returned box,
**I want** to scan each slab and match its cert to a card by intake id, a cert already held elsewhere refused by name, with counters of scanned, matched, ungraded and upcharges, and to finish so every submission in the batch is ready at once with its pickup code emailed,
**so that** a slab can never be handed to the wrong collector and nobody is told ready twice.

### grade10-admin-grading-batches-US-03: Operator records what did not come back as drawn

**As a** member of shop staff finishing a batch,
**I want** a card on the manifest but not in the box recorded as held by the grader with its expected date or as not returned, a damaged slab photographed before it leaves the box, and the collector emailed the same day either way,
**so that** every exception is a fact on one card with the money it changes.

### grade10-admin-grading-batches-US-04: Operator re-estimates a batch that is running late

**As a** member of shop staff reading the grader's order status in the morning,
**I want** to type the grader's stage onto the batch, set a new estimate with a reason, and have every collector in it emailed the day I set it,
**so that** the shop tells the collector before the collector asks.

### grade10-admin-grading-batches-US-05: Operator keeps the safe under its cap

**As a** member of shop staff building a batch,
**I want** the tiles to read what is closing, what is with graders and past its estimate, what is back unchecked, and the declared value in the safe against its cap,
**so that** the shop never holds more than it is covered for.

### grade10-admin-grading-batches-US-06: Operator saves a half-received batch and finishes later

**As a** member of shop staff called away mid-scan,
**I want** the batch to keep its scans and its exceptions until I come back,
**so that** the collectors are told once, when receiving is finished, and never on a half-checked box.

### grade10-admin-grading-batches-US-07: Grader moves a card up a level on its invoice

**As** the grader,
**I want** a card worth more than the level allows charged at the next level on my invoice and the difference recorded on that card at receiving,
**so that** I am paid as invoiced and the collector is told the day the grades post.

### grade10-admin-grading-batches-US-08: Grader keeps one card back and ships the rest

**As** the grader,
**I want** to keep a card for a further look with an expected date and ship the rest of the batch,
**so that** the shop hands the others back and one card stays with me without a dispute.

### grade10-admin-grading-batches-US-09: Grader runs past its published time

**As** the grader,
**I want** the shop to quote my published time plus two weeks from the ship day and to carry my stages in my words,
**so that** waiting on me never reads as waiting on the shop, and a late batch is the shop's re-estimate rather than my promise.
